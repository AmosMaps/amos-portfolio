About

I work where statistics, engineering and the business meet — the part of the job where a model only counts once it runs on a schedule, survives bad input, and lands in a dashboard somebody actually opens on Monday morning.

Today I'm a Data Scientist at ADEI (PTY) Ltd, where I build and deploy WhatsApp chatbot solutions for debt collection companies, micro-lenders and municipalities. These bots are wired into banking APIs, third-party bill payment gateways and caller/voice systems, so a customer can go from a reminder message to a completed payment without a human in the loop. Behind them sit pipelines I designed to capture, validate and store every interaction for analytics and compliance reporting — plus monitoring that catches failures before a client does.

Before that, as a Junior Portfolio Analyst and Python Developer, I built end-to-end automation frameworks: SACRRA-compliant fixed-width file submissions, API integrations, and standalone executables with scheduling, logging and error handling. I've spent enough time with sensitive financial and personal data to treat validation as a feature, not an afterthought — automated data-quality checks and resubmission controls are the difference between a compliance report you trust and one you re-run by hand.

I hold a BSc Honours in Statistics from the University of Limpopo and completed ExploreAI Academy's Higher System Development programme in Data Science. My day-to-day toolkit is Python, SQL and Power BI, with Pandas, Scikit-Learn and MLflow on the modelling side, and SQL Server and AWS underneath.
